# Socratic: 60-second demo video

**Goal:** show that an instructor can turn course notes into an AI tutor in minutes, and that a student gets a real, voice-led lesson with tracked progress.

**Total length:** 60s (about 150 spoken words at a calm pace)
**Format:** screen recording + voiceover (optional small face-cam in the corner for the intro/outro)

---

## Clips to record

Record each clip separately, at 1080p, with a clean browser (hide bookmarks, close other tabs, use the made-up demo data). Record a few seconds extra at the start and end of every clip so you can trim.

| # | Clip | What to do on screen | Raw length | Used in video |
|---|------|----------------------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Hook / intro** | Landing page or logo. Optional: face-cam saying the hook line. | 10s | 0:00 to 0:06 |
| 2 | **Instructor: create a tutor** | Click create tutor, type name + subject (e.g. "Chemistry 101"), save. | 15s | 0:06 to 0:14 |
| 3 | **Instructor: upload in teaching order** | Drop 3 files, use the up/down buttons to reorder, confirm. Show them processing to READY. | 20s | 0:14 to 0:24 |
| 4 | **Instructor: course outline + studio** | Open the studio: show the lesson outline (rename/reorder one lesson) and the metrics panel. | 15s | 0:24 to 0:30 |
| 5 | **Student: sign in and pick a tutor** | Sign in, show the tutor picker with progress bars on each course. | 10s | 0:30 to 0:36 |
| 6 | **Student: voice lesson** | Open a tutor. Let it start the lesson itself. Show the voice orb and the slide board following the audio. Interrupt it once with a question. | 30s | 0:36 to 0:46 |
| 7 | **Student: wrong answer feedback** | Answer a check question wrongly on purpose. Capture the tutor explaining why and giving the right answer. | 15s | 0:46 to 0:50 |
| 8 | **Progress + outline** | Open the course outline: "Lesson 6 of 8 · 72%", per-lesson percentages, overall bar. | 10s | 0:50 to 0:54 |
| 9 | **Break and resume** | Press "take a break" (or say it), then say "I'm back" and show it resumes in the same place. | 15s | 0:54 to 0:57 |
| 10 | **Outro** | Logo or landing page, with your name and repo/URL on screen. Optional face-cam. | 8s | 0:57 to 1:00 |

### Optional extra clips (b-roll, only if time allows)
- Text chat mode streaming an answer with Markdown/LaTeX math (3 to 5s).
- Pacing control switching Normal / Relaxed / Slow (3s).
- Out-of-scope question being redirected back to the course (5s).

### Recording tips
- Make sure the demo tutor already has a lesson partly done, so the progress bar shows something other than 0%.
- Pre-upload one set of files and have them READY. For clip 3, record the reorder step live, then cut to the finished state.
- Use a decent mic for the voiceover; record it separately after the screen clips, and match the timing in the editor.
- Zoom the browser to about 125% so text is readable on phones.
- Add captions. Many people watch with sound off.

## Script (voiceover)

Timings are approximate. Aim for about 2.5 words per second.

**[0:00 to 0:06] Clip 1: Hook**
> "What if every course had its own AI tutor, one that only teaches from your notes? This is Socratic."

**[0:06 to 0:14] Clip 2: Create a tutor**
> "As an instructor, I create a tutor for my course: a name, a subject, and how I want it to teach."

**[0:14 to 0:24] Clip 3: Upload in order**
> "Then I upload my notes. PDFs, Word, Markdown, whatever I have. I put them in teaching order, confirm, and they're processed into lessons."

**[0:24 to 0:30] Clip 4: Outline and studio**
> "Each document becomes a lesson I can rename or reorder, and I can see usage and feedback metrics for the tutor."

**[0:30 to 0:36] Clip 5: Student picker**
> "Students sign in, pick a tutor, and see how far through each course they are."

**[0:36 to 0:46] Clip 6: Voice lesson**
> "The tutor starts the lesson by itself, by voice. A slide board follows along, and I can interrupt any time to ask a question."

**[0:46 to 0:50] Clip 7: Wrong answer**
> "When I get a check question wrong, it tells me why and explains the right answer."

**[0:50 to 0:54] Clip 8: Progress**
> "Every lesson has its own percentage, plus an overall course bar."

**[0:54 to 0:57] Clip 9: Break and resume**
> "Need a break? Say so. It saves my place and picks up exactly where I left off."

**[0:57 to 1:00] Clip 10: Outro**
> "Socratic: an AI tutor for every course. Built by [your name]."
